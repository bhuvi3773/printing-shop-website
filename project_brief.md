# PROJECT BRIEF — PRINTING SHOP WEBSITE

## 1. Project Overview

Build a professional, responsive website for a printing business that offers visiting cards, flex printing, and other printing-related services.

The website must help customers discover available services, select their printing requirements, view applicable prices, and contact the shop to discuss or confirm their orders.

The website must be easy to navigate, reliable, mobile-friendly, and straightforward for the business to maintain.

The visual template and design direction will be selected and approved separately by the project owner. Do not make independent design decisions before receiving those instructions.

## 2. Primary Objectives

The website must:

1. Present the printing business and its services clearly.
2. Help customers find the printing product they need.
3. Allow customers to select relevant product specifications.
4. Calculate and display prices using verified business pricing rules.
5. Allow customers to enquire about their selected products.
6. Provide the shop's contact information and location.
7. Work correctly on mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers.
8. Be completed and tested within the agreed development deadline.

## 3. Target Customers

The intended customers may include:

- Individuals who need visiting cards or personal printing.
- Small businesses and retailers.
- Offices and professionals.
- Event organizers.
- Educational institutions and students.
- Companies requiring promotional materials.
- Product sellers needing labels or stickers.

The final service catalogue must reflect the actual services offered by the printing business.

## 4. Required Website Sections

### 4.1 Home

Include:

- Business introduction.
- Main call-to-action for exploring services.
- A direct path to the pricing section or calculator.
- A summary of the main printing services.
- A preview of previous work.
- A clear way to contact the shop.

### 4.2 Services

Organize available printing services into understandable categories.

Potential categories include:

- Visiting cards and business stationery.
- Flex banners and signboards.
- Flyers, pamphlets, posters, and brochures.
- Stickers and product labels.
- Digital printing and document services.
- Invitations and other custom printing.

Only include services confirmed by the business. Do not represent unavailable services as active offerings.

### 4.3 Product Selection and Customization

Customers must be able to select a printing service and configure the relevant options.

Depending on the product, these options may include:

- Product type.
- Dimensions or size.
- Quantity.
- Material or paper type.
- Printing type, including colour or black and white where applicable.
- Single-sided or double-sided printing.
- Finish and other available options.

Display only options that are relevant to the selected service.

The available options and their valid combinations must be based on the actual capabilities of the printing business.

### 4.4 Pricing Calculator

Provide a pricing calculator that displays the estimated price for a customer's selected requirements.

Requirements:

- Calculate the price from verified business rates and approved pricing rules.
- Update the displayed estimate when relevant selections change.
- Validate quantities, dimensions, and other required inputs.
- Display the selected product specifications alongside the estimate.
- Show the total price and, when appropriate, the unit price.
- Include additional charges only when they are supported by the business's actual pricing rules.
- Handle minimum quantities, quantity-based rates, and size-based pricing where applicable.
- Display a quotation-request option when the final price cannot be calculated reliably.
- Clearly identify estimates that require confirmation from the shop.

Never invent actual prices or present demonstration values as genuine business rates.

If the required price data is unavailable, identify the missing information and provide a way for the customer to request a quote.

### 4.5 Our Work / Gallery

Include a section showcasing examples of the business's printing work.

Requirements:

- Organize examples by relevant product categories where practical.
- Use real business photographs or approved assets when available.
- Do not claim that sample or placeholder images represent completed customer work.
- Allow customers to browse examples without interfering with the main enquiry flow.

### 4.6 About the Business

Include the business introduction and any verified information about its experience, capabilities, and services.

Do not invent company history, customer testimonials, certifications, awards, or business claims.

### 4.7 Contact and Enquiry

Provide the business's confirmed contact details, including applicable information such as:

- Phone number.
- WhatsApp contact.
- Physical shop address.
- Opening hours.
- Map or location link.

Do not invent contact details or operating hours.

Provide a clear enquiry path for customers who need additional information or a custom quotation.

## 5. Customer Journey

Implement the following customer journey:

1. The customer visits the website.
2. The customer explores the available printing services.
3. The customer selects a product.
4. The customer chooses the applicable product specifications.
5. The website calculates and displays the estimated price when sufficient verified pricing information is available.
6. The customer reviews the selected specifications and price.
7. The customer contacts the shop through WhatsApp, phone, or another approved enquiry method.
8. The customer and shop discuss or confirm the order directly.
